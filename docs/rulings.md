# RULING log: how we read an unclear rule

Not DEVIATIONS.md (what the engine gets wrong). One line per ambiguity:
`RULING: <rule> | <what we did> | <why> | <Status> | <source>`.
Status words: **Confirmed** (a primary source says so), **Corrected** (newer source wins),
**Unsourced** (our reading; no source found), **Fan-source**.

- **R1** RULING: End of game | Game ends after a turn with anyone at 10+ Souls or 5+ Wounds; highest Souls−Wounds wins; tie → highest XP | RB (2023) is newer than the FAQ, which says 5 Wounds loses outright and ties go to the lowest XP | Corrected | RB p19 vs FAQ Seq 5c
- **R2** RULING: Priority | Strict LIFO stack; any player may respond; no active-player primacy; the active player is simply asked first in each window | The 10th Anniversary edition removed active-player primacy | Corrected | RB p3, RB p9 vs FAQ Top Five
- **R3** RULING: Starting discard pile | 4 random Rooms + 2 random Spells face up in one shared discard pile at setup | RB is silent; FAQ states it; many base cards take from the discard pile | Confirmed (FAQ) | FAQ Seq 1
- **R4** RULING: Empty Room or Spell deck | Shuffle that type's cards out of the discard pile into a new deck; if none, nothing is drawn | Neither source covers it | Unsourced | —
- **R5** RULING: Build-phase windows | Each player's build turn is a priority window: the active player is asked first and may also place their Room; others may cast; the turn ends when everyone passes in a row with an empty stack. One more window follows the last build turn, before the reveal | Turns FAQ Seq 2a into discrete windows | Unsourced (shape), Confirmed (who may act) | FAQ Seq 2a
- **R6** RULING: "a Hero in (a/your) dungeon" | Means the Hero currently in a Room of that dungeon; Heroes waiting at the entrance are not in the dungeon | FAQ distinguishes entrance from dungeon | Confirmed | FAQ Another Castle, FAQ Fear, FAQ Trepidation
- **R7** RULING: Teleportation | The Hero leaves its current Room (the exit death check applies), then enters the first Room again, keeping its damage | FAQ: keeps damage and continues | Unsourced (exit check) | FAQ Teleportation
- **R8** RULING: Out of Heroes | If the Hero deck is empty and no Hero is in town or at an entrance at the end of a turn, the game ends and is scored per R1 | Otherwise the game cannot end | Unsourced | —
- **R9** RULING: Searching the Hero deck (Seducia) | The ordinary and Epic parts are reshuffled separately so Epics stay at the bottom | Preserves RB p10 deck structure | Unsourced | RB p10, RB p23 Search
- **R10** RULING: Motivation | The extra Room can only be placed during your own build turn; resolved after your build turn ended it does nothing | FAQ: declared and placed with your first Room | Confirmed | FAQ Motivation
- **R11** RULING: Triggered abilities | "When you build", "if a Hero dies in this Room", "when another Room is destroyed" and Level Up resolve at once, in order, without using the stack; only Spells and activated abilities use the stack | RB p9 speaks of Spells and abilities being played | Unsourced | RB p9, RB p20 Activated Ability
- **R12** RULING: The Crushinator (BMR032) | Uses the wiki's revised text ("once per turn") | Later printing | Fan-source | wiki
- **R13** RULING: Random discards / takes (BMR011, BMR012, BMR038) | Drawn by the seeded RNG from the chosen type | FAQ: the chooser picks from concealed cards, which is a random pick | Confirmed | FAQ Specter's Sanctum, FAQ Succubus Spa, FAQ Torture Chamber
- **R14** RULING: Deactivated Room in the Hero's path | Treated as a blank space: no damage, no ability, no window | RB: ignore its damage and abilities; treat as a blank space | Confirmed | RB p21 Deactivate
- **R15** RULING: Single opponent | Effects that say "choose an opponent" still ask, even with one opponent | Series rule: never assume a choice | Confirmed | PLAN D9
- **R16** RULING: Hero entering the boss | A Hero that leaves the last Room alive reaches the Boss and deals its Wound at once | RB p17 | Confirmed | RB p17
- **R17** RULING: "Heroes at the entrance" during another player's processing | Spells can only target Heroes inside a Room; the Hero at the door can be targeted only by town/entrance effects | R6 | Confirmed | FAQ Another Castle
