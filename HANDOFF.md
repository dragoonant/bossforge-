## How a rule flows

A step on `s.queue` (e.g. `enterRoom`) applies one rule through a door (`BF.roomDamage`), then
queues a priority `window`. The window asks each player in turn (active player first), skipping
players with nothing playable. A cast goes through `declare` (targets, then cost) onto `s.stack`;
when everyone passes, `resolveTop` runs the card's `effect`.
